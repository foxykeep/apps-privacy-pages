FoxyJam — Delete account & data

App by foxykeep · Privacy policy

FoxyJam is a jam-tracking app for Android. Sign-in with Google is optional. This page explains how to delete your cloud account data or individual jam data.

Delete some data (keep your account)

While signed in or offline, you can delete data inside the app:

  • Jam batches — cancel in-progress batches or remove completed batches from the app
  • Inventory — use jar counts until a batch is finished, or delete the batch

When you are signed in, deletions sync to your private cloud backup (Firebase Firestore).

Stop cloud sync (sign out)

  1. Open FoxyJam → Settings
  2. Tap Sign out

Your jams remain on the device. Cloud data is not deleted by signing out.

Request full account & cloud data deletion

To delete all jam data stored in the cloud for your Google account, email us from the same address you use with Google Sign-In:

foxyjam@foxykeep.com

Subject line suggestion: FoxyJam account deletion request

We will delete your Firestore documents under users/{your-user-id}/ within 30 days and confirm by email. No extra retention period beyond backup systems we may use during deletion.
